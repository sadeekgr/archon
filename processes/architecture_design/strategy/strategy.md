## Approach

- Build from the fundamentals upwards, understanding each piece before adding the next.
- Terms must be general and precise, and must not carry meanings the model does not intend.
- Reason about each element independently first, and compare with the literature only afterwards.
- The literature can carry the assumptions of present-day organisations, for example that search only answers known problems or serves profit. Such assumptions are to be wary fo. Where they conflict with the design, if considered biased or incorrect, the design takes priority, otherwise they are incorporated.
