Define starting architecture: strategy, research, development, archive
Inside strategy: action_plan, unknowns
Inside research: processes, projects (both are assuming such things are needed)
	Inside processes: architecture_design, to_research (both are assuming such things are needed)

In strategy.md: Define purpose, objective, in a concise, clear manner, in a way that makes it clear what success looks like.
Brainstorm and come up with various ideas and approaches and perspective and related fields and concepts. Then pass that all to Claude and ask for references, research paper, current state and precedents.
Download all and store in references folder inside archive as md files, then summarise each paper inside summaries folder inside references folder.
Then after downloading all that might be needed or beneficial, should compare all between each other and cut down all that is not needed, or is covered in other papers, or there is more recent results that are more relevant or just better alternatives.
Then synthesise/summarise the summaries into a synthesis.md file inside references folder.
Each paper's summary should start with a concise reason as to why that paper is important or relevant.
The summaries.md should suggest which to read first, then roughly divide the summaries by category, how they help, what field or aspect they contribute on.
Synthesis.md should have different aspects and categories needed or regarding the project and very concisely what we have to consider from the summaries (should use all the summaries). Should also have tests and other numerical of note for comparison (better explained later) and things to be wary of (warnings) and open questions that the project could address.
Read through synthesis.md and most promising summaries.md.
Define the design principles, scope and presentation inside strategy.md. Define overall design idea with all of those things in mind, and foundation elements (most fundamental and essential and impactful components).
Register all into action_plan and unknowns. Decide whether to start with the action plan or unknowns. Better to have the clearest picture, so usually prioritize unknows first, each one that is chosen for research moves into to_research and it might or not open a project inside the projects folder inside research folder.
For action_plan, it should always start with foundations, get them right and then build, all according to approaches and strategy defined in strategy.md.
Pick one foundation element, generate best possible design and compare against all relevant summaries and aspects to find flaws, differences (make decisions whether to incorporate difference or stick to current design), and improve it until it is fully done, completed, and adherent with all the approaches defined in strategy.md.
If design or things are unsure or experimental, have tests and real world examples or behaviours to validate results and behaviour against, even a behaviour from a different field that should manifest in this project (you can model the project so that it can be compared to that, throughout the whole project this should be done to various parts and their interactions).
Repeatedly follow action_plan until all is built.
