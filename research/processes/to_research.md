## Parked design questions

Questions raised while defining the foundations and set aside until the pieces they depend on are defined.

- **Agent-owned tools and benefits:** tools currently belong to the role, and an agent who leaves the role leaves its tools. Tools owned by the agent, and work benefits in general, are left out until there is a reason to think they change results.
- **How hard each tool is to change:** improving a tool and replacing it are both choices, and some tools are much harder to change than others. The cost and difficulty of each kind of change still need to be defined.
