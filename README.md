# ARCHON

A simulator for organisational design.

## What it is

ARCHON represents an organisation as people, roles and the connections between them: who reports to whom, who communicates with whom, who decides what. It runs the organisation under realistic conditions and measures how well its structure meets given criteria.

## Why

Structure decides which information reaches which decision. People act on partial, delayed and sometimes distorted views of reality, while the consequences of their decisions fall on reality itself. Two organisations with the same people and resources can therefore perform very differently. ARCHON makes that difference visible and measurable.

## What it will do

- Model an organisation, simulate it, and evaluate its structure against one or several criteria: money, knowledge, skill, equality and well-being, resilience to shocks, and more.
- Compare alternative structures under identical conditions.
- Grow from the smallest organisation to large multi-level ones, ultimately to the scale of a city.
- Later, search for and recommend better structures.
- Show it all through an interactive interface: the organisation from frontline to leadership, information and work moving through it, and measures alongside.

## Status

Early stage. The foundations of the architecture are being designed; nothing is implemented yet.
