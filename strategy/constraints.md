## Many kinds of criterion, several at once

Performance depends on the criterion, and many kinds should be possible, alone or together:

- reaching a given technology, solution or body of knowledge, or a technological level;
- reaching a given amount of money;
- reaching and keeping a level of equality or well-being over the years;
- reaching given skill levels for everyone in every field;
- resisting shocks and other negative events;
- being able to take advantage of beneficial events.

This should show what each organisation does naturally, which is best when the criterion is made its aim, and how giving it that aim changes the organisation.

## City scale

The model must grow from the smallest organisation to a city, and be ready for what cannot be foreseen now.
