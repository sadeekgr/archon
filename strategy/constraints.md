What the design must leave room for. These are known possibilities that are not yet committed to, or just general constraints to follow. Each entry says what it is and what it asks of the design now. Entries are ordered by how early in the build they must be allowed for. An entry that is committed to moves to the action plan.

## Many kinds of criterion, several at once

Performance depends on the criterion, and many kinds should be possible, alone or together:

- reaching a given technology, solution or body of knowledge, or a technological level;
- reaching a given amount of money;
- reaching and keeping a level of equality or well-being over the years;
- reaching given skill levels for everyone in every field;
- resisting shocks and other negative events;
- being able to take advantage of beneficial events.

This should show what each organisation does naturally, which is best when the criterion is made its aim, and how giving it that aim changes the organisation.

Asks of the design:

- What the observers judge by is kept separate from what the organisation strives towards.
- The state is kept per agent and over time, not only as totals.
- Events, harmful and beneficial, can be part of the conditions an organisation is run under.
- Measures are reported side by side, not merged into one score.

## City scale

The model must grow from the smallest organisation to a city, and be ready for what cannot be foreseen now.

Asks of the design:

- No element assumes a small number of agents or a single level of organisation.
- Where scale changes a value, it is a setting, not a fixed number.

## Competitors and other outside agents

Other organisations and outside agents may be modelled later.

Asks of the design:

- Terms and elements do not assume the organisation is alone.
