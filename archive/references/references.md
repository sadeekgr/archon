# References — Markdown versions

One Markdown file per held reference, named like its source file in `references_raw`. Each file opens with the citation, a link to its source, how it was converted, and what was checked. Digital PDFs were converted from their text layer with pymupdf4llm; scanned PDFs were OCR'd with Tesseract 5.2 at 300 dpi, with paragraph and column order taken from Tesseract's layout analysis, and where the scan carried its own text layer the OCR was cross-checked against it. Page markers `*[pdf p. N]*` give the PDF page position. Figures are omitted; tables and equations are approximate; JSTOR download stamps were removed.

**Checks.** *Title matched*: the title words from the citation were found on the first pages. *Pages with text*: pages that yielded fewer than 30 words are listed, with the reason (blank page, figure). *Word count against the text layer*: for digital PDFs, the converted words as a fraction of what the PDF itself contains. *Stopword ratio*: share of the 25 commonest English function words, about 0.25–0.40 for clean prose, lower for tables, code and references, near zero for garbage. *Tesseract confidence*: mean per-word confidence, 0–100. *Agreement with the text layer*: share of the source layer's distinct words (4+ letters) that the OCR also produced. PASS means every check cleared its threshold; CHECK lists what did not and was then inspected by hand.
